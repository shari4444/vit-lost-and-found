Academic Intake & Property Retention Protocol

All recovered items are officially logged into the central asset registry within 4 hours of submission. High-value electronics, wallets, and government identities are deposited into secured vaults at the Campus Security Directorate. Items remain claimable for 90 academic days before state institutional transfer.
